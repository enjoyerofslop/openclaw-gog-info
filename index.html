openclaw-gog

A private Google Workspace connection for a personal assistant.

This application connects its operator's Google account to a locally managed OpenClaw assistant through the gog command-line client. It supports requested email and calendar tasks, document and spreadsheet workflows, and personal organization.

It is for the operator's own use. It is not a public service and does not offer account registration. This information site does not request Google access or collect Google account data.

What the connection does

The assistant can prepare calendar and inbox summaries and perform other tasks authorized by its operator. The operator grants Google permissions through Google's consent screen and can revoke them through the Google account's connections settings.
