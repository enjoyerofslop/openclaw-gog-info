openclaw-gog

Privacy notice

Updated October 7, 2026.

openclaw-gog is a personal-use connection between its operator's Google account and a locally managed OpenClaw assistant. It is not offered as a public service.

Google data and purpose

Depending on the permissions granted and the task requested, the connection can access email content and metadata, mail settings, calendar events, Drive files, documents, spreadsheets, contacts, tasks, and basic account identity. These permissions support the operator's requested communication, scheduling, document, and organization tasks. Granted permissions do not mean all available data is routinely retrieved.

Processing and sharing

The connection runs through the gog client on the operator's computer. Relevant task content may be passed to the AI model providers configured in OpenClaw. Summaries or requested results may be delivered through the operator's selected messaging services. Those services process the information under their applicable policies. Google account content is not published on this information site.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in the operating system's credential store. Retrieved content or summaries may appear in local task records, conversation history, memory, and backups, and in the selected delivery service. Retention depends on the operator's settings and the services involved. Revoking Google access stops future access but does not automatically erase existing records or backups.

Control and deletion

The operator can revoke this app's access in Google Account connections, stop related automations, and remove retained local records. For access or deletion questions, contact tumboassistant837@gmail.com.

This website

These are static information pages. They contain no application analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or sign-in form. The hosting provider may record ordinary request information such as IP addresses in accordance with its own privacy policy.
